Good Questions

Plain answers about financing.

Does checking my options affect my credit score?

No. Checking your options through Hearth uses a soft credit check that does not affect your score, and it takes about a minute. If you go on to accept an offer, the lending partner sets the rates, terms and any further steps.

Is Posada the lender?

No. Posada is not a lender. Financing is available through Hearth (NMLS ID 1628533), which works with a network of lending partners, and rates and terms are set by the lending partner. Your loan agreement is with that partner. Your proposal, remodeling agreement and warranty are with Posada.

Can I combine financing with the design retainer?

Yes. The design retainer is $250 per bathroom or $500 per kitchen, and it is credited to your project once the agreement is signed. Financing changes how you pay for the project; it does not change the retainer or the credit. The retainer is step 02 of our process.
