The kitchen features Winter Garden homeowners ask for.

By Posada Remodeling & Design · April 29, 2026 · Updated October 18, 2026 · 9 min read

In Short

The kitchen features Winter Garden homeowners ask for most are a larger island, better storage, custom cabinetry, updated countertops and improved lighting, followed by open layouts, pantry storage and a textured backsplash. Choose features by how the kitchen is used every day, not by trend.

These are the kitchen features Winter Garden homeowners ask for most, and what each one involves. Knowing them can help you plan a kitchen remodel that feels more useful, polished, and personal.

Larger kitchen islands

One of the most requested kitchen remodeling features is a larger island. A well-designed island can completely change the way a kitchen functions.

Homeowners often want an island that can be used for meal preparation, casual seating, serving food, extra storage, homework, work-from-home space, and entertaining guests.

In many homes, the kitchen is connected to the main living area, so the island becomes a natural gathering place. A larger island can help the kitchen feel more open while also creating a better flow between cooking, dining, and everyday living.

For a kitchen remodel, the island should be planned carefully. Size, walkway clearance, seating space, storage, lighting, and countertop material all matter. A beautiful island should not only look good. It should make the kitchen easier to use.

Better kitchen storage

Storage is one of the biggest reasons homeowners start a kitchen renovation. Even a nice-looking kitchen can feel frustrating if there is not enough room for cookware, small appliances, pantry items, dishes, and everyday essentials.

Popular kitchen storage upgrades include deep drawers for pots and pans, pull-out trash and recycling cabinets, pantry cabinets, spice pull-outs, tray dividers, appliance garages, drawer organizers, corner storage solutions, and built-in shelving.

Better storage helps reduce clutter and makes the kitchen feel more organized. For busy families, where the kitchen is often used throughout the day, storage planning can make a major difference.

A good kitchen remodel should look at how the homeowner actually uses the space. The right cabinet layout can improve function without making the kitchen feel crowded.

Custom cabinetry

Custom cabinetry is one of the most important features in a high-quality kitchen remodel. Cabinets affect the look, storage, layout, and overall feel of the kitchen.

Many homeowners want cabinets that feel more tailored to the home instead of basic or builder-grade. This may include taller cabinets, soft-close drawers, custom inserts, decorative panels, glass cabinet doors, a built-in pantry, or a more furniture-style island.

Popular cabinet features include soft-close doors and drawers, tall upper cabinets, custom pantry storage, built-in drawer organization, hidden trash pull-outs, decorative end panels, glass display cabinets, under-cabinet lighting, and custom island cabinetry.

In a full kitchen remodel, custom cabinetry can help create a more polished and functional space. It also gives homeowners more flexibility with storage, layout, and design style.

Updated countertops

Countertops play a major role in both the look and performance of a kitchen. Homeowners want surfaces that are attractive, durable, and easy to maintain.

Quartz remains one of the most popular choices because it offers a clean, refined look with strong durability. It works well for busy kitchens because it resists staining and is available in many colors and patterns.

For general countertop material guidance, homeowners can also review the Natural Stone Institute’s stone care resources when comparing surface options.

Popular countertop priorities include durability, easy maintenance, light and bright colors, natural stone-inspired designs, waterfall island edges, large uninterrupted prep areas, and coordinated backsplash designs.

The right countertop can make a kitchen feel more finished and elevated. It should also match the way the kitchen is used every day.

Improved kitchen lighting

Lighting is one of the most overlooked parts of a kitchen remodel, but it makes a huge difference. A kitchen can have beautiful cabinets and countertops, but poor lighting can make the entire space feel dull or difficult to use.

Homeowners often want a layered lighting plan that includes recessed ceiling lights, pendant lights over the island, under-cabinet lighting, decorative fixtures, task lighting near prep areas, and softer lighting for evening use.

Good lighting improves both function and atmosphere. It helps with cooking, cleaning, entertaining, and showcasing the finishes in the kitchen.

In a kitchen renovation, updated lighting can also make the space feel larger, brighter, and more modern.

Open and functional layouts

Many homeowners want their kitchen to feel more open and connected to the rest of the home. Older kitchens can feel closed off, cramped, or disconnected from the dining and living areas.

A better kitchen layout can improve traffic flow, cooking space, storage access, seating, visibility into nearby rooms, entertaining space, and everyday comfort.

Open layouts are also among these features, especially when homeowners want the kitchen to feel more connected to the main living area.

Not every kitchen needs a fully open-concept design, but the layout should feel practical and comfortable. Sometimes the biggest improvement comes from relocating appliances, adjusting cabinet placement, adding an island, or opening a wall when appropriate.

A successful kitchen remodel should balance beauty with real-life function.

Walk-in or built-in pantry storage

Pantry storage is another major feature homeowners want. A well-planned pantry can make the entire kitchen easier to keep organized.

Depending on the home, this may include a walk-in pantry, tall pantry cabinets, pull-out pantry storage, built-in shelving, small appliance storage, dry goods organization, or hidden storage near the kitchen.

For families, a better pantry setup can reduce countertop clutter and make daily routines easier. Even if the home does not have space for a large walk-in pantry, custom cabinetry can often create better storage than the original layout.

Backsplash with style and texture

A backsplash is more than a wall covering. It is one of the best places to add style, texture, and personality to a kitchen.

Popular backsplash options include subway tile, herringbone patterns, marble-look tile, handmade-look tile, full-height backsplash, neutral textured tile, and clean modern tile layouts.

Many homeowners want a backsplash that feels timeless but still adds character. The right backsplash can bring the cabinets, countertops, hardware, and lighting together.

For a refined kitchen design, the backsplash should complement the rest of the finishes instead of competing with them.

High-quality hardware and fixtures

Small details can make a big difference in the finished look of a kitchen. Hardware, faucets, sinks, and fixtures help complete the design.

Popular finish choices include brushed gold, matte black, polished chrome, brushed nickel, champagne bronze, and mixed metal accents.

Homeowners often want hardware and fixtures that feel updated but not overly trendy. These details should match the cabinet style, lighting, countertops, and overall design direction.

In a custom kitchen remodel, the finishing touches are what help the space feel intentional and complete.

Durable flooring

Kitchen flooring needs to handle daily use, spills, foot traffic, pets, and family activity. Homeowners want flooring that looks beautiful but can also stand up to real life.

Popular kitchen flooring options include porcelain tile, luxury vinyl plank, engineered wood, wood-look tile, and large-format tile.

The best flooring depends on the home, budget, lifestyle, and design goals. In many kitchen remodeling projects, homeowners want flooring that connects the kitchen with nearby living areas for a more cohesive look.

Durable flooring can help the remodeled kitchen feel clean, updated, and easier to maintain.

Better appliance integration

Appliances are a major part of any kitchen remodel. Homeowners often want appliances that fit the layout better and make cooking easier.

Popular appliance-related upgrades include built-in microwave placement, range hood upgrades, panel-ready appliances, larger refrigerator space, beverage coolers, improved oven placement, better outlet planning, and cleaner appliance spacing.

Appliance planning should happen early in the kitchen design process. The size and location of appliances can affect cabinetry, countertops, electrical work, ventilation, and overall layout.

A kitchen remodel should make the appliances feel like part of the design instead of an afterthought.

Refined finish details

The best kitchen remodels are often defined by the details. Trim, crown molding, cabinet panels, lighting placement, outlet locations, hardware alignment, grout color, and finish transitions all affect the final result.

Homeowners often want kitchens that feel polished, clean, and well planned. That comes from careful attention to the details, not just selecting nice materials.

Refined finish details may include crown molding, finished cabinet panels, coordinated hardware, clean tile transitions, well-placed lighting, matching trim details, thoughtful color combinations, and smooth paint and finish work.

These details help create a kitchen that feels complete and professionally designed.

How to choose the right features

The best kitchen features depend on your home, lifestyle, budget, and goals. Some homeowners want a more open layout with a large island. Others care most about storage, cabinetry, lighting, or updated finishes.

Before starting a kitchen remodel, it helps to think through how you use your kitchen every day, whether you need more storage, whether you want a larger island, whether you are changing the layout, what style you want, which materials matter most, and what your ideal timeline and budget range looks like.

A thoughtful plan can help you avoid rushed decisions and create a kitchen that works better long term.

Frequently asked questions

What are the most popular kitchen features homeowners want?

Popular kitchen features include larger islands, better storage, custom cabinetry, quartz countertops, updated lighting, stylish backsplashes, durable flooring, and refined finish details.

Is a kitchen island worth adding during a remodel?

Yes, a kitchen island can add prep space, seating, storage, and a better gathering area. The island should be sized correctly for the kitchen layout so it improves function without blocking traffic flow.

What kitchen upgrades add the most function?

The most functional kitchen upgrades usually include better cabinet storage, improved lighting, a more efficient layout, durable countertops, pantry storage, and better appliance placement.

What should I consider before starting a kitchen remodel?

Before starting a kitchen remodel, consider your layout, storage needs, design style, material selections, budget, timeline, and how your family uses the kitchen every day.

Do custom cabinets make a big difference?

Yes, custom cabinetry can improve storage, layout, appearance, and overall kitchen function. It allows the kitchen to be tailored to your home instead of relying on a standard cabinet layout.

What is the best style for a Winter Garden kitchen remodel?

The best style depends on the home and homeowner. Popular styles include transitional, modern, traditional, farmhouse, coastal, and contemporary kitchen designs.
