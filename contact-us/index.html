After You Reach Out

Three steps,
no surprises.

You will always know what happens next, who is in your home, and what it costs. Here is what to expect once you send the form or call.

Prefer to look around first? See finished kitchens and bathrooms, read how our process works, or check typical costs and timelines in the FAQs. We work across Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Lake and Volusia counties; see all service areas.

  1. 01

    We reply within 24 hours

    A member of our team reads your note and photos, then calls or texts to ask a few questions and set a time that suits you.

  2. 02

    A complimentary in-home consultation

    We meet at your home, listen to your ideas, look at the space and tell you honestly which budget range your project falls into.

  3. 03

    An itemized proposal

    When you are ready to move forward, you receive a line-item proposal: labor, materials and timeline, with the upgrades you want and nothing you do not.

Evening and weekend consultations are available. Choose the best time to reach you in the form, and we will plan the visit around your family.
