Kitchen remodel financing options in Ocoee and Orlando

Some homeowners use savings, some use the equity in their home, and some work with their own bank or credit union, and we are happy to work with whichever you choose.

Savings

Paying from savings avoids borrowing costs. Your payment schedule is set out in your agreement, so you know when each payment is due.

A home equity loan or line of credit (HELOC)

Both borrow against the equity in your home, through your own bank or credit union. A home equity loan pays out a lump sum; a line of credit lets you draw funds as the project moves. Your home secures the loan, and your lender sets the rate and terms.

A personal home improvement loan

An unsecured loan from a bank, credit union or online lender. It does not use your home as collateral, and the lender sets the rate, terms and approval.

An FHA Title I home improvement loan

A home improvement loan insured by the Federal Housing Administration and made through approved lenders. HUD sets the program rules, so ask an approved lender, or check HUD.gov, for current limits.

How to compare kitchen remodel loans

  • APR, not just the rate. The APR shows the total cost of borrowing.
  • Fixed or variable. A fixed rate stays the same; a variable rate can change.
  • Total repaid. Compare what you repay over the life of the loan, not only the first bill.
  • Timing. Have financing in place before your start date; each lender sets its own approval timeline.

How much to finance

The right amount depends on your full project scope. These are the three kitchen ranges we quote in Ocoee and the Orlando area in 2026:

  • Refresh & update: $15,000 – $25,000 (cabinet refacing, countertops, fixtures)
