What types of bathrooms do you remodel in Mount Dora?

Primary bathrooms, guest bathrooms, powder rooms, walk-in showers and full custom bathroom spaces for homeowners in Mount Dora: from a tub-to-shower conversion with frameless glass to a spa-style primary suite.

Do you offer bathroom remodeling services in Mount Dora, FL?

Yes. Posada designs and builds bathrooms in Mount Dora and in Eustis, Tavares, Sorrento and Apopka from our studio in Casselberry, with our own team on site and one project manager. Nearby city pages: Apopka, Clermont, DeLand.

Should I choose a walk-in shower or a tub for my Mount Dora bathroom remodel?

It depends on your layout, your household and your long-term plans. Some Mount Dora homeowners want the openness of a walk-in shower; others want the comfort of a tub, and a primary suite can hold both. For the pros and cons, see our walk-in shower vs tub guide.

How long does a bathroom remodel take in Mount Dora?

Industry guides put a typical bathroom remodel at about four to six weeks of construction, depending on size, selections and complexity; larger custom renovations take longer. Ordering tile, vanities and glass before demolition is what keeps a Mount Dora schedule.

Can you help with bathroom design and finish selections?

Yes. We guide Mount Dora homeowners through layout, tile, vanities, countertops, fixtures, lighting and mirrors so the finished room fits the rest of the house, with renderings before anything is ordered. The $250 design retainer is credited once you sign.

Do you offer financing for bathroom remodeling projects?

Financing is available through Hearth (NMLS ID 1628533), which works with a network of lending partners, including 0% APR promotional options for qualified homeowners. Checking your options takes about a minute and uses a soft credit check that does not affect your score. Posada is not a lender; rates and terms are set by the lending partner.
