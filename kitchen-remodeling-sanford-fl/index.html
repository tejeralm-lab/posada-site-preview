What Moves the Number

Six choices that set your range.

  • Cabinetry grade: in-stock, semi-custom or fully custom
  • Countertop material: quartz or granite, slab size and edge profile
  • Layout changes: plumbing, electrical, walls or a new island
  • Size: linear feet of cabinetry and square feet of countertop
  • Finish level: backsplash, lighting, hardware and a custom wood hood
  • Structural or permit requirements

Good Questions

Sanford kitchen questions.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Sanford?

Most Posada kitchens fall into three ranges: refresh and update, $15,000 to $25,000; full remodel, $25,000 to $50,000; luxury and open-concept, $50,000 to $80,000 and up. Ranges reflect typical Central Florida projects in 2026. Your written proposal is exact. We tell you which range yours falls into at the consultation.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Industry guides put a midrange kitchen remodel at roughly four to eight weeks of construction, and a full remodel with layout changes at eight to twelve weeks or more. Custom cabinetry adds lead time; in-stock collections turn around in one to two weeks.

Do you help with kitchen design and layout planning?

Yes. We help with layout planning, cabinetry, countertops, backsplash, flooring and lighting, with renderings before anything is ordered. The $500 kitchen design retainer is credited once the agreement is signed.

What is included in a kitchen remodel?

Depending on the project: design planning, demolition, cabinetry, countertops, backsplash, flooring, lighting, fixtures, painting and trim work, every line itemized in your proposal.

Do I need a permit for a kitchen remodel in Sanford?

Only for some work. Rules vary by jurisdiction: in Orange County, for example, moving plumbing, electrical or walls needs a permit, while replacing cabinets and countertops in the same layout does not. Inside Sanford city limits, permits go through the City of Sanford Building Division; addresses outside the city limits go through Seminole County. Permits included when your project needs one; we coordinate it through our licensed general contractor partner. Our Florida remodel permit guide covers the details.
