Good Questions

Before you call.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Orlando?

Most Posada kitchens fall into three ranges: a refresh at about $15,000 to $25,000, a full remodel at $25,000 to $50,000, and a luxury kitchen with custom cabinetry at $50,000 to $80,000 and up. Your exact number depends on layout, cabinetry and finishes, which is why you receive an itemized written proposal before any work begins.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

A bathroom refresh typically runs $8,000 to $15,000, a full bathroom remodel $15,000 to $30,000, and a spa-style primary suite with a custom walk-in shower $30,000 to $40,000 and up. Tub-to-shower conversions, custom vanities and designer tile are priced line by line in your proposal, so you know what each choice costs before you decide.

How long will my remodel take?

It depends on scope. Industry guides put a midrange kitchen remodel at roughly four to eight weeks of construction, and a full remodel with layout changes at eight to twelve weeks or more. Most bathroom remodels finish in one to four weeks. Custom cabinetry and countertop fabrication add lead time, so your schedule is set once your selections are final.

Do I need a permit, and who handles it?

Not always. Orange County, for example, does not require a permit for new finished flooring or for replacing cabinets and countertops in the same layout, while moving plumbing, electrical or walls does. When your project needs a permit, it is included: we coordinate it through our licensed general contractor partner, so you do not manage the paperwork or inspections yourself.

Can we stay in our home during the remodel?

Most families do. A kitchen or bath is out of service while the work happens, so we walk through what to expect and the order of work at your consultation. Clients regularly note in their reviews that the team was respectful of their home, cleaned up at the end of each day and communicated what was planned next.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Financing is available through Hearth, which works with a network of lending partners, including 0% APR promotional options for qualified homeowners. Checking your options takes about a minute and uses a soft credit check, so it does not affect your score. Posada is not a lender, and rates and terms are set by the lending partner.
