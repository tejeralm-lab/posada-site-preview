Planning a bathroom remodel in Central Florida and wondering what it will actually cost? Most Central Florida bathrooms fall into three ranges: a refresh and update at $8,000 to $15,000, a full remodel at $15,000 to $30,000, and a spa or primary suite at $30,000 to $40,000 and up. Your final bathroom remodel cost comes down to the size of your bathroom, the materials you choose, and how much of the layout you change. Below we break down real 2026 pricing for Central Florida, what drives the cost up or down, how long the work takes, and whether you’ll need a permit.

Where the money goes: a cost breakdown

It helps to understand how a bathroom remodel budget is split. For a typical mid-range project, costs break down roughly like this:

CategoryShare of budget
Labor & installation20–35%
Fixtures (tub, shower, toilet, faucets)15–20%
Tile & flooring10–15%
Vanity & cabinetry10–15%
Plumbing & electrical10–15%
Countertops5–10%
Demo, prep & permits5–10%

What affects the cost of your bathroom remodel

  • Size of the bathroom. More square footage means more tile, materials, and labor.
  • Moving plumbing or changing the layout. Relocating a toilet, tub, or shower is one of the single biggest cost drivers.
  • Material quality. Stock vs. custom vanities, porcelain vs. natural stone, standard vs. frameless glass.
  • Fixtures. A basic tub/shower combo costs far less than a custom walk-in shower or freestanding soaking tub.
  • Accessibility or structural changes. Curbless showers, widened doorways, or grab bars add cost.
  • Condition of existing plumbing and electrical. Older homes sometimes need updates behind the walls.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

Industry guides put most bathroom remodels at one to four weeks of construction, with primary suites that move plumbing or walls at the longer end. Be sure to add time up front for design and material lead times, since custom vanities, specialty tile, and glass enclosures often need to be ordered in advance.

Do you need a permit to remodel a bathroom in Florida?

In most cases, no. Most bathroom remodeling — new tile, a vanity, fixtures, and finishes — updates what is already there and does not require a permit. Permits come into play mainly with structural changes, such as removing a load-bearing wall, or exterior changes, such as adding or enlarging a window. Permit rules and fees vary by jurisdiction: Orange County, Seminole County, and individual cities such as Winter Park and Longwood each have their own building departments. Posada Remodeling & Design is a design-build remodeling studio. When a project does need a permit, we coordinate it through our licensed general contractor partner, who pulls the permit and oversees that portion of the job, so it stays with the right professional and is completed to the Florida Building Code.

How much does a small bathroom remodel cost?

A small bathroom remodel in Central Florida typically lands in the $8,000 to $15,000 refresh band. Because the footprint is smaller, you save on tile and materials, but labor doesn’t shrink at the same rate, since the same plumbing, waterproofing, and finish work still has to happen in a tighter space. Smart material choices and keeping the existing layout are the best ways to keep a small bathroom remodel affordable.

If the goal is a bigger, safer shower rather than a full renovation, a targeted tub-to-shower conversion usually lands in the refresh-and-update band, $8,000 to $15,000, and industry guides put it at one to two weeks.

Bathroom remodel FAQs

What is the average cost to remodel a bathroom in Orlando, Florida?

Most Orlando and Central Florida bathrooms fall into three ranges in 2026: $8,000 to $15,000 for a refresh and update, $15,000 to $30,000 for a full remodel, and $30,000 to $40,000+ for a spa or primary suite. A small bathroom or a tub-to-shower conversion usually lands in the refresh range.

What does a $10,000 bathroom remodel look like?

$10,000 sits in the refresh and update range, $8,000 to $15,000. That is where most tub-to-shower conversions and small-bathroom updates land, with the layout and plumbing kept where they are. Refreshing rather than replacing where possible, and choosing durable mid-range finishes, keep a remodel in that range.

Is $5,000 enough to remodel a bathroom?

For a full bathroom remodel, usually not: the refresh and update range starts at $8,000. A smaller scope may fit, and at a complimentary consultation we tell you honestly what your budget covers.

What is the most expensive part of a bathroom remodel?

Labor and fixtures are usually the two largest line items, but the single biggest cost driver is moving plumbing. Relocating a toilet, tub, or shower means opening walls and floors, so keeping your existing layout is the easiest way to control the budget.

Is remodeling a bathroom a good investment?

Yes. A well-executed bathroom remodel is consistently one of the highest-return home improvements, both for resale value and day-to-day enjoyment. Updated bathrooms are also one of the first things Central Florida buyers look for.

Can I remodel a bathroom on a small budget?

Absolutely. Keeping the existing layout, refreshing rather than replacing where possible, and choosing durable mid-range finishes can keep a remodel in the $8,000–$15,000 range. Financing through Hearth is also available; Posada is not a lender.
