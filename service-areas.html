Where We Work

Four counties, one team.

Posada is a family-operated kitchen and bath design and remodeling studio in Casselberry, Florida. From there we design and build across greater Orlando: Orange and Seminole counties first, with Osceola and Lake counties beyond them.

Wherever your home is, the project is handled the same way: a complimentary in-home consultation, an itemized written proposal, and our own team on site with Mike overseeing the work.

  • Orange County: Orlando, Winter Park, Winter Garden, Windermere, Dr. Phillips, Lake Nona, Ocoee, Apopka and Maitland
  • Seminole County: Casselberry, Altamonte Springs, Longwood, Lake Mary, Heathrow, Sanford, Oviedo and Winter Springs
  • Lake County: Clermont, Minneola, Sorrento, Mount Dora and Eustis
  • Osceola County: the communities just south of Orlando
West Orlando homeowners: design consultations in Winter Garden, by appointment. Visits to our Casselberry studio are also by appointment.
