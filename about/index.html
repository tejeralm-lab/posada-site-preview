Meet the Owner

Quality was never optional.

Posada Remodeling & Design is a veteran-owned, family-operated kitchen and bath design and remodeling studio in Casselberry, owned and led by Mike Posada. It is fully insured, BBB Accredited with an A rating, and rated 5.0 on Google with 60+ five-star reviews.

“Choosing a remodeler is a decision about trust. You are welcoming a team into the heart of your home, and that is something we never take lightly.”

Mike Posada learned the craft working alongside his father. The standard he learned there is the one every Posada project is still held to.

Military service came next, then a career in law enforcement. Posada Remodeling & Design was built on what those years taught him: discipline, accountability and respect for the people you serve.

With more than fourteen years of remodeling experience, Mike personally oversees every kitchen, bathroom and flooring project the studio takes on, from the first in-home consultation to the follow-up after completion.

“No shortcuts. No rushed decisions. Just craftsmanship you can trust.”

— Mike Posada

What We Stand For

Four things every project is built on.

“My promise to you is simple: honest numbers, clear communication, and craftsmanship we are proud to sign our name to.”Mike Posada, Owner & Founder

I

Honest numbers

At the first consultation we tell you which budget range your project falls into. Your proposal then itemizes labor, materials and timeline line by line, so you know where every dollar goes before you sign.

II

Design before demolition

Layout, materials and finishes are settled with renderings, so you see the finished room before anyone touches a wall. See every step of our process.

III

A respected home

Our own team, one point of contact, and a clean site at the end of every day. You always know who is in your home and what happens next.

IV

Craftsmanship we sign our name to

A three-year labor and installation warranty on every project, complete written warranty terms before you sign, and a follow-up from Mike after completion.
