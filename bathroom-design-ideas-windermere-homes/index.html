Bathroom design ideas for Windermere homes.

By Posada Remodeling & Design · March 26, 2026 · Updated October 18, 2026 · 5 min read

In Short

Windermere homeowners planning a bathroom remodel usually want a brighter, more open room: a walk-in shower with a clean design, tile with texture, a vanity with real storage and better lighting. The best results come from designing around how the household uses the room, with finishes that stay timeless.

Bathroom design ideas for Windermere homes can make a major difference when planning a remodel that feels both beautiful and functional. If you are planning a bathroom remodel in Windermere, one of the most important parts of the process is choosing a design that feels both beautiful and functional. A well-designed bathroom should not only look elevated, but also make everyday routines easier, more comfortable, and more enjoyable.

In many Windermere homes, bathrooms are becoming more than just practical spaces. Homeowners are looking for designs that feel brighter, more open, and more refined while still fitting the character of the home. Whether you are updating a guest bathroom or transforming a primary suite, the right design choices can make a major difference in the final result.

If you are planning a bathroom remodel in Windermere, thoughtful design choices can help create a space that feels both beautiful and functional.

Here are some of the ideas homeowners ask us about most.

Create a bright, open feel

One of the most popular goals in bathroom remodeling is making the space feel larger and more open. Even if the square footage stays the same, thoughtful design choices can completely change how the room feels.

The best of these ideas combine comfort, style, and practical everyday function.

This can often be done with:

  • light, neutral color palettes
  • larger mirrors
  • glass shower enclosures
  • improved lighting
  • cleaner, more streamlined finishes

A bright and open bathroom tends to feel more timeless and more luxurious, especially in a primary bath.

Choose a walk-in shower with a clean design

Walk in showers continue to be one of the most requested bathroom upgrades. They help modernize the space, improve accessibility, and create a cleaner overall look.

They remain one of the most popular features in modern bathroom remodeling projects because they improve both style and function.

Many homeowners in Windermere prefer walk in showers with features like:

  • frameless glass
  • large format tile
  • recessed niches
  • linear drains
  • rainfall shower-heads
  • bench seating

These details can make the bathroom feel more custom while also improving everyday use.

Use tile to add character and texture

Tile plays a major role in the final look of a bathroom. It can make the design feel simple and elegant or bold and dramatic depending on the material, size, and layout you choose.

Some popular bathroom tile ideas include:

  • large format porcelain tile for a clean modern look
  • marble look tile for a more upscale feel
  • textured tile for feature walls or shower areas
  • warm wood look flooring for a softer, more natural appearance
  • classic patterns like herringbone or stacked layouts for added interest

The key is choosing tile that works with the overall style of the home and does not feel too trendy too quickly.

Upgrade the vanity for style and storage

The vanity is often one of the main focal points in a bathroom. It also plays a big role in how functional the space is day to day.

A well-designed vanity can help improve:

  • storage
  • counter space
  • organization
  • visual balance
  • overall design style

For Windermere homes, many bathroom remodels include vanities with cleaner lines, better drawer storage, upgraded countertops, and more refined hardware and fixture selections. Double vanities are especially popular in primary bathrooms where both comfort and convenience matter.

Bring in better lighting

Lighting can completely change how a bathroom looks and feels. Many older bathrooms feel dark or dated simply because the lighting is not working well for the space.

A more updated lighting plan may include:

  • vanity lighting on both sides of the mirror
  • recessed ceiling lights
  • decorative sconces
  • layered lighting for a softer, more polished feel

Good lighting is not just about appearance. It also makes the bathroom more functional for daily routines like getting ready in the morning or winding down at night.

Add luxury through small details

Not every bathroom needs a massive layout change to feel high end. In many cases, smaller design upgrades can make the biggest difference.

Some examples include:

  • upgraded plumbing fixtures
  • frameless shower glass
  • custom mirrors
  • built in shower niches
  • floating shelves
  • cleaner trim details
  • higher-end hardware
  • a more cohesive finish palette

These details help the bathroom feel intentional and complete rather than pieced together.

Design the space around how you live

A great bathroom design is not just about what looks good in pictures. It should also fit the way you actually use the space.

That might mean:

  • more counter space
  • better drawer storage
  • a larger shower instead of a tub
  • easier to clean materials
  • improved layout and flow
  • a more comfortable setup for a shared bathroom

The most successful bathroom remodels balance appearance with practicality. That is especially important if you are planning to stay in your home long term.

If you are also updating nearby spaces, explore our kitchen design ideas for Windermere homes for more inspiration.

Keep the style timeless

It can be tempting to follow every new trend, but bathrooms usually look best when the design feels balanced and lasting. Timeless design does not mean boring. It means making choices that will still feel attractive and relevant years from now.

Modern versions of these ideas often include walk in showers, upgraded vanities, and layered lighting.

That often includes:

  • neutral foundational materials
  • simple but elegant finishes
  • quality craftsmanship
  • thoughtful layout decisions
  • a design that complements the rest of the home

In a place like Windermere, homeowners often want spaces that feel elevated without looking overdone. That balance is where good design matters most.

These ideas work best when they balance style, function, and long-term appeal.

Many homeowners also want their bathrooms and kitchens to feel cohesive, especially when planning kitchen remodeling in Windermere.
