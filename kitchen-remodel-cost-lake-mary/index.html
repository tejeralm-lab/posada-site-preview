Kitchen Remodel Cost Lake Mary (2026 Local Pricing Guide)

By Posada Remodeling & Design·March 4, 2026·Updated July 21, 2026·4 min read

If you’re planning a kitchen remodel in Lake Mary, one of the first questions you probably have is: How much will it cost? Kitchen remodeling prices depend on kitchen size, cabinet choices, and materials. They also depend on needed electrical, plumbing, or structural changes.

Many homeowners in Lake Mary, Heathrow, and Alaqua Lakes start their remodel by researching typical local pricing. Below is a clear guide to help you understand what most local homeowners spend when remodeling their kitchens.

Builders built many kitchens in Lake Mary homes between the late 1990s and early 2000s. As a result, homeowners often remodel to update layouts, improve storage, and install modern materials. Popular upgrades include larger kitchen islands, quartz countertops, improved lighting, and better cabinet organization. These changes make the kitchen more functional for daily use and entertaining.

Average Kitchen Remodel Cost in Lake Mary

Most kitchen remodeling projects in Lake Mary fall into three investment ranges.

Entry-Level Kitchen Remodel

$25,000 – $35,000

This type of remodel works well when the existing layout stays the same and the focus is on updating finishes.

Typical upgrades include:

  • new cabinetry
  • new countertops
  • updated flooring
  • new lighting fixtures
  • new appliances

These updates improve both the look and functionality of the kitchen without major construction.

Mid-Range Kitchen Remodel

$35,000 – $50,000

This is the most common remodeling range for Lake Mary homeowners.

Projects in this category often include:

  • semi-custom cabinets
  • quartz or granite countertops
  • tile backsplash
  • improved lighting layout
  • minor electrical updates
  • small plumbing changes

This level of remodeling balances quality upgrades with long-term value.

High-End Kitchen Remodel

$55,000+

High-end remodels usually involve larger upgrades such as:

  • custom cabinetry
  • layout changes
  • removing a load-bearing wall
  • creating an open-concept kitchen
  • premium stone countertops
  • luxury appliances
  • upgraded flooring throughout

These remodels completely transform the space and create a modern kitchen designed for long-term use.

Kitchen Remodel Cost Per Square Foot

In Lake Mary, kitchen remodels typically average:

$175 – $325 per square foot

  • The total cost depends on the quality of the cabinets.
  • It also depends on the countertop material.

Costs can change depending on how many structural or layout changes you make during construction.

What Affects Kitchen Remodeling Costs the Most?

Cabinets

Cabinetry is usually the biggest expense and can represent 30–40% of the total budget. Custom cabinets increase costs but also provide more design flexibility.

Layout Changes

Keeping the same kitchen layout helps control costs. Moving plumbing, electrical lines, or removing walls increases labor and construction work.

Countertops

Quartz is a popular countertop choice in Florida because it is durable, low maintenance, and modern looking.

Labor and Installation

Experienced installation ensures proper fit, accurate measurements, and long-term durability.

Kitchen Size and Pricing

Kitchen size also affects remodeling costs. Larger kitchens require more cabinets, countertops, flooring, and labor.

In Lake Mary homes, kitchens typically range from 150 to 300 square feet. Kitchens with large islands, extended counter space, or extra storage will naturally increase the total project investment.

Do You Need Permits for Kitchen Remodeling in Lake Mary?

Permits are typically required when a remodel includes:

  • load-bearing wall removal
  • major electrical upgrades
  • major plumbing relocation
  • structural changes to the home

Cosmetic upgrades like cabinets, countertops, flooring, and backsplashes usually do not need permits. This applies if you are not changing structural, electrical, or plumbing systems.

When your kitchen project does require a permit, we coordinate it through our licensed general contractor partner, who pulls the permit and oversees that portion of the work. Posada Remodeling & Design is a design-build remodeling studio; permits go through our licensed general contractor partner, so that part stays with the right professional – and you do not have to chase it.

Return on Investment

Kitchen remodeling is one of the most valuable home improvements. Many homeowners recoup 60–80% of the project cost when they sell their home. This depends on the finishes and overall design.

Updated kitchens also help homes sell faster in competitive markets like Lake Mary.

FAQ – Kitchen Remodel Cost in Lake Mary

What’s the typical timeline for a kitchen renovation?

Most projects take 4–8 weeks depending on the scope of work.

Which part of a kitchen remodel usually ends up being the priciest?

Cabinets and labor are usually the largest expenses.

Is $25,000 sufficient to remodel a kitchen?

Yes. Entry-level remodels that keep the same layout often fall within this range.

Does removing a wall always require a permit?

No. Only load-bearing wall removal or structural changes require permits.
