How Much Does a Kitchen Remodel Cost in Central Florida? (2026 Guide)

By Mike Posada·July 6, 2026·Updated September 28, 2026·4 min read

Planning a kitchen remodel in Central Florida and wondering what it will cost? Most Orlando-area homeowners spend between $25,000 and $60,000 on a full kitchen remodel in 2026. Smaller updates start around $15,000, while large open-concept or luxury kitchens can run $80,000 or more. Your final kitchen remodel cost depends on the size of the kitchen, the materials you choose — especially cabinets — and how much of the layout you change. Below we break down real 2026 pricing for Central Florida, what drives the cost up or down, how long the work takes, and whether you’ll need a permit.

Average kitchen remodel cost in Central Florida (2026)

Here’s what Orlando-area homeowners are typically investing, based on the size and scope. These kitchen remodel cost ranges reflect real 2026 projects:

Kitchen typeTypical cost (Central FL, 2026)

Standard kitchen$25,000 – $50,000
Large / open-concept kitchen$50,000 – $80,000

Where the money goes: a cost breakdown

For a typical mid-range kitchen, the budget splits roughly like this:

CategoryShare of budget
Cabinetry25–35%
Countertops10–15%
Appliances10–20%
Labor & installation20–30%
Flooring7–10%
Lighting, electrical & plumbing8–12%

What affects your kitchen remodel cost

  • Cabinets — stock vs. semi-custom vs. fully custom is the single biggest cost lever.
  • Countertops — laminate, quartz, or natural stone.
  • Appliances — standard vs. professional-grade.
  • Layout changes — moving plumbing, gas, or removing a wall adds cost. See our guide to open concept kitchen remodel costs if you are thinking about opening the space up.
  • Size — more cabinets, counters, and flooring.
  • Existing conditions — older wiring or plumbing may need updating.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

A typical Central Florida kitchen remodel takes 4 to 8 weeks of active work once materials are on site. Minor refreshes can wrap in 2–3 weeks, while large or fully custom kitchens — especially with moved plumbing or removed walls — can take 8 to 10 weeks. Custom cabinets and stone countertops have the longest lead times, so order early.

Do you need a permit to remodel a kitchen in Florida?

In most cases, no. Most kitchen remodeling — new cabinets, countertops, tile, flooring, and fixtures — updates what is already there and does not require a permit. Permits come into play mainly with structural changes, such as removing a load-bearing wall, or exterior changes, such as adding or enlarging a window or door. Rules vary by jurisdiction: Orange County, Seminole County, and cities like Winter Park and Longwood each have their own building departments. Posada Remodeling & Design is a design-build remodeling studio. When a project does need a permit, we coordinate it through our licensed general contractor partner, who pulls the permit and oversees that portion of the job, so it stays with the right professional and is completed to the Florida Building Code.

How much does a 10×10 or small kitchen cost?

The 10×10 kitchen is the industry’s standard pricing benchmark (about 100 sq ft with ~20 linear feet of cabinets). In Central Florida, a 10×10 kitchen remodel typically runs $20,000 to $45,000. Keeping the existing layout and choosing semi-custom cabinets are the best ways to control a small kitchen remodel cost.

Kitchen remodel cost FAQs

What is the average cost of a kitchen remodel?

For a standard kitchen in Central Florida, expect roughly $25,000 to $50,000 in 2026, depending on size and finishes.

What is the most expensive part of a kitchen remodel?

Cabinetry is almost always the largest expense — typically 25–35% of the budget — followed by countertops and appliances.

Can I remodel a kitchen on a budget?

Yes. Keeping the layout, refacing or choosing semi-custom cabinets, and durable mid-range counters keep costs down — and financing through Hearth spreads the cost over time.
