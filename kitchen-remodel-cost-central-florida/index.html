Planning a kitchen remodel in Central Florida and wondering what it will cost? Most Central Florida kitchens fall into three ranges: a refresh and update at $15,000 to $25,000, a full remodel at $25,000 to $50,000, and a luxury or open-concept kitchen at $50,000 to $80,000 and up. Your final kitchen remodel cost depends on the size of the kitchen, the materials you choose — especially cabinets — and how much of the layout you change. Below we break down real 2026 pricing for Central Florida, what drives the cost up or down, how long the work takes, and whether you’ll need a permit.

What affects your kitchen remodel cost

  • Cabinets — stock vs. semi-custom vs. fully custom is the single biggest cost lever.
  • Countertops — laminate, quartz, or natural stone.
  • Appliances — standard vs. professional-grade.
  • Layout changes — moving plumbing, gas, or removing a wall adds cost. See our guide to open concept kitchen remodel costs if you are thinking about opening the space up.
  • Size — more cabinets, counters, and flooring.
  • Existing conditions — older wiring or plumbing may need updating.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Industry guides put a midrange kitchen remodel at four to eight weeks of construction, and a full remodel with layout changes at eight to twelve weeks or more; custom cabinetry adds lead time before that. Custom cabinets and stone countertops have the longest lead times, so order early.

Do you need a permit to remodel a kitchen in Florida?

In most cases, no. Most kitchen remodeling — new cabinets, countertops, tile, flooring, and fixtures — updates what is already there and does not require a permit. Permits come into play mainly with structural changes, such as removing a load-bearing wall, or exterior changes, such as adding or enlarging a window or door. Rules vary by jurisdiction: Orange County, Seminole County, and cities like Winter Park and Longwood each have their own building departments. Posada Remodeling & Design is a design-build remodeling studio. When a project does need a permit, we coordinate it through our licensed general contractor partner, who pulls the permit and oversees that portion of the job, so it stays with the right professional and is completed to the Florida Building Code.

How much does a 10×10 or small kitchen cost?

The 10×10 kitchen is the industry’s standard pricing benchmark (about 100 sq ft with ~20 linear feet of cabinets). A same-layout 10x10 kitchen usually lands in the full-remodel band, $25,000 to $50,000. Keeping the existing layout and choosing semi-custom cabinets are the best ways to control a small kitchen remodel cost.

Getting the best value — and financing your remodel

Kitchen remodel cost FAQs

What is the average cost to remodel a kitchen in Orlando, Florida?

There is no single average, because scope moves the number most. Most Orlando and Central Florida kitchens fall into three ranges in 2026: $15,000 to $25,000 for a refresh and update, $25,000 to $50,000 for a full remodel, and $50,000 to $80,000+ for a luxury or open-concept kitchen. A standard kitchen usually lands in the full-remodel range.

Is $30,000 enough for a kitchen remodel?

For many kitchens, yes. $30,000 sits inside the full-remodel range, $25,000 to $50,000, where a same-layout 10x10 kitchen usually lands. Moving walls or plumbing, fully custom cabinetry or a larger room push the number higher.

Is $10,000 enough for a kitchen remodel?

Usually not for a full kitchen. The refresh and update range starts at $15,000, so $10,000 typically covers a smaller scope than new cabinetry, counters and lighting throughout. At a complimentary consultation we tell you honestly which range your kitchen falls into.

What is the most expensive part of a kitchen remodel?

Cabinetry is almost always the largest expense — typically 25–35% of the budget — followed by countertops and appliances.

Can I remodel a kitchen on a budget?

Yes. Keeping the layout, refacing or choosing semi-custom cabinets, and durable mid-range counters keep costs down — and financing through Hearth spreads the cost over time.
