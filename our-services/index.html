The Design Phase

Every detail decided before demolition.

The design phase turns your ideas into a plan that balances beauty, function and precision, whether for a kitchen remodel, a bathroom renovation or new flooring.

01

Design retainer

A retainer of $250 per bathroom or $500 per kitchen reserves our design time. It is credited to your project once the agreement is signed.

02

Line-item proposal

A written proposal itemized line by line: labor, materials and timeline. Pick the upgrades you want and leave out anything you don’t.

03

Select your materials

Cabinetry, countertops, tile, fixtures and flooring, chosen with our interior design advisor. Studio visits in Casselberry by appointment.

04

Design & renderings

Layout, materials and finishes come together in renderings, so you see your new room before any work begins.
