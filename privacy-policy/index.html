Text Messaging (SMS) Privacy

Posada Remodeling (POSADA REMODELING INC) collects your mobile phone number when you give it to us on our website contact form, by texting or calling us, or during an in-home estimate. We use it only to text you about your project, estimate, scheduling, and customer service. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile app

This section covers the Posada Remodeling app for iPhone. Tejera Industries Inc. publishes the app on the App Store and runs it for Posada Remodeling. Everything else in this policy also applies to the app.

What the app collects

If you use the app without signing in:

  • If you request a consultation: your name, phone, email, the project address, what the project is, your note, and the times that suit you. We use these to call you and set up a visit.
  • If you approve a proposal: the name you type, the time, your IP address and your device type. We keep these as the record of your signature.
  • If you watch one of our videos: it plays from YouTube using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player, so YouTube receives your device’s internet address.

If you are a Posada client and sign in:

  • Your email address, so we can send you a six-digit sign-in code.
  • What you do in the app for your project: messages to our team, photos you send, selections you choose, finishes you heart, and extra work you approve or decline. We record when you have read our messages.

If you are on the Posada team and sign in:

  • Your name, email, role and password. We store only a scrambled form of your password, never the password itself.
  • The work you record: daily logs, hours, punch list items, tasks, leads, photos, receipts, walkthrough videos and room scans.
  • Questions you ask Lisa, our assistant, by voice or typing.

Phone features the app uses

  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to take or pick a photo. We receive only the photos you pick.
  • Microphone (team only): only while you record a question for Lisa or a walkthrough video.
  • Room scanning (team only, iPhone Pro): makes a 3D model and measurements of a room, saved with the job.
  • Face ID and Touch ID: if you turn them on to sign in, your iPhone does the check. We never receive your face or fingerprint.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, we save a notification token for your phone so we can tell you about your project.
  • Location: the app does not ask for or use your location.

Your sign-in is kept in your iPhone’s secure keychain. For team members, the app keeps a copy of the jobs on the phone so it works with no signal. Signing out removes it.

Outside services

We use these companies to run the app. Each one gets only what it needs to do its job for us:

  • Vercel: hosts our servers and stores photos, videos, room scans and files.
  • Supabase: our database.
  • Google (Gmail): sends sign-in codes, appointment reminders and project emails.
  • Expo and Apple: deliver notifications and app updates. A notification can include a name and the first words of a message.
  • YouTube (Google): plays our videos.

AI services (team features)

Some team features send information to AI companies. Before each feature sends anything for the first time, the app names the company and asks for the team member’s permission. A team member can take that permission back in the app under Account.

  • AssemblyAI turns voice recordings into text: questions asked to Lisa, and the sound of walkthrough videos.
  • Anthropic (Claude) answers questions asked to Lisa, and drafts proposals from a walkthrough video or a typed description.
  • Microsoft reads Lisa’s answers out loud.

Walkthrough videos can show the inside of a client’s home. They are used only to write that client’s proposal.

What we don’t do

We show no ads and sell no information. We don’t track you across other apps or websites. The app has no analytics or advertising tools.

How long we keep it

We keep project information for as long as we work with you and as long as we need it for our records, warranties and taxes. Signed proposals, agreements and invoices are business records we must keep.

Deleting your information

  • Clients: in the app, go to Account > Delete my account. This deletes your sign-in straight away. Your project records stay with Posada, as described above.
  • To ask us to delete more: email info@posadaremodeling.com or call (407) 319-3503. We’ll confirm, and finish within 30 days. We’ll also ask the outside services listed above to delete what they hold, and keep only what the law requires, such as signed agreements and invoices.

Children

The app is for homeowners and our team. It is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.
