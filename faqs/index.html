01 · Cost & Financing

What it costs, and how to pay for it.

Typical 2026 ranges for Central Florida projects, how financing through Hearth works, the design retainer explained, and planning several rooms as one project.

See financing options
How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Orlando and Central Florida?

Kitchen remodels fall into three typical ranges. A refresh and update runs $15,000 to $25,000. A full remodel runs $25,000 to $50,000. A luxury or open-concept kitchen runs $50,000 to $80,000 and up. Ranges reflect typical Central Florida projects in 2026. Your written proposal is exact, itemized line by line, so you see what each choice costs before you decide. Our kitchen remodeling page explains what moves the number.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

A bathroom refresh and update typically runs $8,000 to $15,000. A full bathroom remodel runs $15,000 to $30,000. A spa-style primary suite runs $30,000 to $40,000 and up. Ranges reflect typical Central Florida projects in 2026. Your written proposal is exact. Tub-to-shower conversions, frameless glass, custom vanities and designer tile are each priced as their own line, and new flooring is itemized by the square foot. See bathroom remodeling for the details.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Financing is available through Hearth (NMLS ID 1628533), which works with a network of lending partners, including 0% APR promotional options for qualified homeowners. Checking your options takes about a minute and uses a soft credit check that does not affect your score. Posada is not a lender; rates and terms are set by the lending partner. You can check your options on our financing page before or after your consultation.

What is the design retainer, and does it count toward my project?

The in-home consultation is complimentary. Design work begins with a design retainer of $250 per bathroom or $500 per kitchen, credited to your project once the agreement is signed. It comes before your proposal, material selections and renderings, so you see your new room on paper before anyone touches a wall. We disclose it in step two of our process, never as a surprise.

Can you remodel the kitchen, bathrooms and floors in one project?

Yes. One team designs, prices and builds every room, and your project manager is your one point of contact through the build. Your line-item proposal lists labor, materials and timeline, so you can see what each part costs and choose the upgrades you want. The design retainer is $250 per bathroom or $500 per kitchen, credited to your project once the agreement is signed. Reviewing three bathrooms and new kitchen floors, Jamie H. wrote that they would 100% work with the team again.

02 · Timeline & Living at Home

How long does a kitchen or bathroom remodel take?

It depends on scope, and your schedule is set once your selections are final. Industry guides put a midrange kitchen remodel at roughly four to eight weeks of construction, and a full remodel with layout changes at eight to twelve weeks or more. The same guides put most bathroom remodels at one to four weeks. Custom cabinetry and countertop fabrication add lead time before work begins, which is one reason we finish the design before demolition.

Can we stay in our home during the remodel?

Most families do. The kitchen or bathroom being remodeled is out of service while the work happens, so at your consultation we walk through what to expect, the order of work and where the crew will be each day. Our own team works in your home, the site is cleaned at the end of every day, and you have one point of contact for the whole project. Reviewing a full kitchen remodel and new floors, Christina A. wrote that the team was respectful to their home.

Who will actually be in my home?

Our own team, led by people you will have already met. Mike Posada, the owner, personally oversees every project, and your project manager is your one point of contact on site, so you always know who is in your home and what happens next. Reviewing a room remodel, Joyce P. wrote that Mike keeps you posted on day and arrival times. After completion, Mike follows up to make sure you are happy with the work. Meet the team.

03 · Design & Materials

Designed before demolition.

Who designs your room, how quickly in-stock cabinetry can arrive, and when a project needs a permit.

See finished projects
Do you handle the design, or do I need my own designer?

We do the design. Posada is a kitchen and bath design and remodeling studio, so layout, cabinetry, counters, tile, fixtures and lighting are chosen with you and shown in renderings before anyone touches a wall. Yani Tejera, our interior design advisor, guides your selections, and the same team that designs your room builds it, so the finish you approve on paper is the finish you live with.

Do you carry in-stock cabinets for a faster turnaround?

Yes. Alongside custom and semi-custom cabinetry in classic overlay and premium inset, we offer in-stock framed and frameless collections with a one to two week turnaround. Shaker doors come in white, grey, pearl, blue, natural, onyx and cashmere, and euro-style doors in glossy white and natural oak. Every cabinet we install carries a lifetime warranty. Ask at your consultation which collection fits your budget range, or read more on our kitchen page.

Do I need a permit, and who handles it?

Not always. Orange County, for example, does not require a permit for new finished flooring or for replacing cabinets and countertops in the same layout. Moving plumbing, electrical or walls does. Permits included when your project needs one; we coordinate it through our licensed general contractor partner. You do not manage the paperwork or inspections yourself, and we tell you at the proposal stage whether your project needs one.

Every project carries a 3-year labor and installation warranty. The cabinetry we install carries a lifetime warranty.
