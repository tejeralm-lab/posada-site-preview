Should I choose a walk-in shower or a tub for my Ocoee bathroom remodel?

It depends on your layout, your household and your long-term plans. Some Ocoee homeowners want the openness of a walk-in shower; others want the comfort of a tub, and a primary suite can hold both. For the pros and cons, see our walk-in shower vs tub guide.

How long does a bathroom remodel take in Ocoee?

Industry guides put a typical bathroom remodel at about four to six weeks of construction, depending on size, selections and complexity; larger custom renovations take longer. Ordering tile, vanities and glass before demolition is what keeps a Ocoee schedule.

Can you help with bathroom design and finish selections?
