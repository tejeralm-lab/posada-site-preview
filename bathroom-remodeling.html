Good Questions

Bathroom questions, answered.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Orlando?

A bathroom refresh typically runs $8,000 to $15,000, a full bathroom remodel $15,000 to $30,000, and a spa-style primary suite with a custom walk-in shower $30,000 to $40,000 and up. Ranges reflect typical Central Florida projects in 2026. Your written proposal is exact: tile, glass, vanity and fixtures are priced line by line, so you know what each choice costs before you decide.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

Industry guides put most bathroom remodels at roughly one to four weeks of construction, with primary suites that move plumbing or walls at the longer end. Custom vanities and frameless glass add fabrication lead time, so your schedule is set once your selections are final. We walk through the order of work at your consultation, so you know when the room will be out of service.

Can you convert my tub into a walk-in shower?

Yes. In a tub-to-shower conversion the tub comes out, the new shower is waterproofed and tiled, and frameless glass finishes it. Keeping the drain and plumbing where they are keeps the scope simple. Moving plumbing, electrical or walls adds scope and typically requires a permit. Permits included when your project needs one; we coordinate it through our licensed general contractor partner.

What warranty comes with a new shower?

Every project carries a three-year labor and installation warranty. Shower waterproofing systems, where included, carry three-year, ten-year and lifetime warranties. You receive complete written warranty terms before you sign, and Posada is fully insured, with a certificate of insurance on request.
