How It Works

Seven steps, one team.

From the first visit to the final walkthrough, the people you meet at the consultation are the people who build your room. Nothing is handed off, and nothing is lost in between.

Typical investment ranges are published on our kitchen and bathroom pages, so you can walk into step one already knowing what projects really cost.

Read the FAQs
  1. 01

    In-home consultation

    We meet at your home, walk the space and listen to what you want to change. Before we leave, we tell you honestly which budget range your project falls into, so you can decide whether to go further. Complimentary, with evening and weekend appointments available.

  2. 02

    Design retainer

    If you would like to move ahead, a design retainer of $250 per bathroom or $500 per kitchen reserves our design time. It is credited to your project once the agreement is signed, so the design work is never an extra cost on top of your remodel.

  3. 03

    Line-item proposal

    You receive a written proposal itemized line by line: labor, materials and timeline. Pick the upgrades you want and leave out anything you don't. Nothing is bundled, and you always know where every dollar goes.

  4. 04

    Select your materials

    Cabinetry, countertops, tile, fixtures and flooring, chosen with our interior design advisor. Visit the Casselberry studio by appointment to see samples in person, including in-stock cabinet collections with a turnaround of one to two weeks.

  5. 05

    Design & renderings

    Layout, materials and finishes come together in renderings, so you see your new room before any work begins. Changes are simple at this stage and costly after demolition, which is why we design first.

  6. 06

    Approve & sign

    When the design and the proposal are right, you sign the agreement. The payment schedule is written into it, and you have your complete written warranty terms before you sign. If you plan to spread the investment over time, financing through Hearth is arranged at this stage.

  7. 07

    Schedule & project manager

    Your start date is set and your project manager is introduced. From here you have one point of contact for every question, and Mike oversees the project personally from the first day to the final walkthrough.

During the Build

A respected home, every day.

A remodel means people in your home for weeks. We hold ourselves to a few simple rules so that time is as easy on your family as it can be.

  • Our own team does the work, led by Mike and your project manager.
  • A clean site at the end of every day.
  • One point of contact who keeps you posted on arrival times and what happens next.
  • Permits included when your project needs one; we coordinate it through our licensed general contractor partner.
  • Fully insured, with a certificate of insurance on request.
